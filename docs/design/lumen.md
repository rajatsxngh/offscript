## Movable conversation — 16 October 2026

At viewport widths of 760 px and above, drag the grip beside the map name to detach the chat from its right-hand dock. Its position is saved per map in this browser, kept on-screen after resizing, and retained when changing topics. Shift plus an arrow key moves the focused handle; Home or the Dock button returns it to the right. Chat drafts and content stay mounted while moving.

The blue tether chooses an exposed edge on both the selected node and panel and follows either as it moves. It hides when the node is obscured, clipped, or overlaps the panel. Floating chat has a more opaque reading surface inside its translucent frame. Below 760 px, chat uses the existing modal layout while retaining the desktop position for later.
